the style block
================

it is not yours, sam: only the user changes it.

the user, 2026-10-06: new priority. the new look is toy-like, grotesque, playful and colourful.
the feel of star birds, but not a copy of it. completely new: take nothing from the old theseus look. images only, no 3D.

1. start every image prompt with the block below, word for word. write only the subject after it.
2. attach 1 or 2 references from `sketches/direction-2026-10-06/` (`starbirds-*`) for the feel.
3. copy no shape from them: no bird, no star birds ship, station, planet, icon or panel.
4. never attach your own earlier output as the style. a new image starts from the block and the references.

## the toy block

    toy-like game art, playful and colourful, like a set of painted vinyl toys in space.
    chunky rounded shapes, grotesque proportions, pushed but not too far: big engines,
    bulbous cockpits, small round windows, fat pipes and bolts. soft toon shading with
    2 or 3 tones and a soft rim light. matte plastic: no gloss, no reflections, no
    texture noise, no grime. small glowing lights. space is dark with small soft stars.
    not photographic, not realistic, no depth of field.

after the block, name one palette below and use only its colours. no old theseus colours: no cream, mustard, teal, olive or rust.

## the palettes

a design variant changes the palette and the shapes, not only one of them.
the user, 2026-10-06: mild, soft pastel tones. greenish pastels are good; strong purple is too much contrast.

- meadow, the first choice: soft red and pastel green, with butter yellow, rose and milk white, on dark forest green space.
- lagoon: aqua and soft pink, with peach and foam white, on dark lagoon teal space.
- postcard: soft blue and soft red, with butter yellow and paper white, on dark slate blue space.
- dusk: lavender, peach and pistachio, mild, on soft dusk purple space. low contrast.
- sherbet: peach, mint, lavender, butter yellow, on midnight blue space.
- bubblegum: pink, sky blue, grape, banana yellow, mint lights, on dark teal space.

dropped, too much contrast: candy, arcade.
